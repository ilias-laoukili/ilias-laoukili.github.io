## Project Overview
"Echoes of Innocence" is a video game scenario developed by Ilias Laoukili and team. The story follows Mathis, a 10-year-old boy searching for his abducted sister, Lina, in a war-torn nation.

Narrative & Setting

The game is set in a devastated district during a civil war. Mathis must navigate 7 distinct environments, from underground hideouts to a rebel camp, solving puzzles and making moral choices.

Core Mechanics

    Exploration: Command-based interaction with the environment.

    Moral Dilemmas: Choices that impact the narrative outcome (e.g., helping others vs. saving resources).

    Survival: Managing stealth to avoid militia patrols.

    Inventory: A weight-limited inventory system with special items like the "Magic-Cookie" that boosts capacity.
