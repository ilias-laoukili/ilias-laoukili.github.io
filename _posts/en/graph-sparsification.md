## My Tremplin Recherche Project
I have been selected for the **Tremplin Recherche 2025/2026 program** to research **Graph Sparsification for Graph Neural Networks (GNNs)**.

The Problem: Oversquashing

GNNs struggle with "oversquashing"—a bottleneck where exponential amounts of information from distant nodes must pass through a few edges, compressing and losing signal. Architectural fixes like attention (GAT) cannot solve this topological flaw.

My Solution: Geometric Restructuring

My research proposes reframing sparsification not as compression, but as geometric intervention. By using discrete Ricci curvature to identify bottleneck edges, we can surgically rewire the graph to improve information flow before learning begins. This shifts the focus from model-driven to geometry-driven optimization.
